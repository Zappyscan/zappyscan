FAQ

Common questions

Can I use just the billing (POS) without other features?
Yes. The Starter plan gives you Zappy POS billing only — no table management, no payroll, no invoicing. It's designed specifically for small cafés, tea shops, and bakeries that just need fast, reliable billing.
Can I add Payroll or Invoice to my existing plan?
Absolutely. You can add any product module to your existing plan as an add-on. Zappy Payroll is +₹499/month, Zappy Invoice is +₹299/month, and Zappy Tasks is +₹199/month — billed along with your existing plan.
Is there a free trial?
Yes — all plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full access to all features in your chosen plan during the trial period.
Do you support Zomato, Swiggy, and Uber Eats?
Yes. Online order integration with Zomato, Swiggy, and Uber Eats is available on the Growth plan and above, and also as a standalone add-on (+₹999/month) for Professional plan customers.
Is Zappy suitable for cloud kitchens and multi-branch restaurants?
Yes. The Enterprise plan is built for cloud kitchens, chains, and multi-branch businesses. It includes a central dashboard, cross-branch reporting, central menu sync, and dedicated onboarding support.
Can I get GST-compliant invoices and purchase orders?
Yes — Zappy Invoice provides fully GST-compliant invoices for B2B clients, purchase orders to vendors, credit/debit notes, and reports ready for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filing. Available as a standalone product from ₹299/month.
